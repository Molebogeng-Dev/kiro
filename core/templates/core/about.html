About iSgela

Teachers, learners, and parents — in one loop.

iSgela is an AI-powered platform for South African schools. When a paper is marked, homework is missed, or a learner is away, the people who need to know find out automatically.

{# ===================================================================== #} {# THE PROBLEM — and how iSgela closes it #} {# ===================================================================== #}

The problem it solves

Schooling has a connection problem, not just a resourcing one — three groups who need to work together often aren't in contact at all.

  • Parents are left out

    Working long hours, many parents can't get to the school to ask how their child is really doing — and find out too late, or not at all.

  • Teachers can't see the pattern

    Marking by hand leaves little time to track why a learner keeps getting something wrong and step in early.

  • Students slip quietly

    Not every child speaks up when they're struggling or skipping work. Without something watching for it, it goes unnoticed.

iSgela closes these gaps by putting the teacher, student, and parent in one connected loop — so a marked paper, a missed assignment, or an absence reaches the right person automatically, instead of depending on everyone finding time to talk.

{# ===================================================================== #} {# WHAT IT DOES — the features #} {# ===================================================================== #}

What it does

  • Scan & Mark

    Photograph a paper or submit homework by camera. AI marks it against the memorandum and explains why an answer was wrong — and how to help.

  • Attendance

    A familiar roll-call for the younger grades, and a quick facial check-in for the older ones. Both land on one shared record.

  • Three connected portals

    Teacher, student, and parent each see what matters to them, in one place, without a separate app for each.

  • Parent notifications

    Plain-language summaries reach a parent the moment their child's work is marked — nothing to log in and go looking for.

  • Progress dashboard

    A whole-school view of marks, attendance, and assignments that flags who needs attention — by clear, checkable rules, not a black box.
